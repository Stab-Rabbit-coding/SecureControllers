## Why these skills, in this repository

`SecureControllers` builds unmanned-system controller hardware (flight controllers,
ESCs, servos, sensor gateways). `control-systems-engineering` (ISA-aligned loop
dynamics, PID tuning, instrumentation and safety) is directly applicable to that work;
`mechanical-engineering`, `statics-and-dynamics`, and `aeronautical-engineering` support
the airframe- and structure-adjacent design work the README's UAV avionics context
implies.
